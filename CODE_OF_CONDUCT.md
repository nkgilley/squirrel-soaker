# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We welcome useful, respectful, and technically curious participation. Assume
good intent, explain disagreements with evidence, and keep reviews focused on
the code and its effects.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
and publishing private credentials or personal media.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. They
will review reports, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary.
